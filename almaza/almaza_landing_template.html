Who we are

Hi, we’re Alfred.

A small family studio in Lebanon. We design and 3D print custom pieces in small batches, right here at home. We started with keepsakes for new parents, and we’d love to print for a brand we grew up with.

Idea 1 · Open Coaster

We’re open to ideas. Literally.

A giant crown cap coaster with a bottle opener printed right into its base. Rest your glass on top, flip it over to open the next one.

Fully printedNo metal insert needed
Your colorsAlmaza red and blue
Idea 2 · Carry On

Brewed by you. Carried by us.

A foldable six pack carrier that folds flat when the bottles are gone. Easy to bring to the match, to the beach or to a friend’s house.

A flat base with ALMAZA on top and a handle that snaps in.

Idea 4 · Your Name Here

Every diamond needs a setting.

A little crown cap name tag that slips over the bottle cap, then swings down on two printed hinges. Nobody mixes up their bottle again. Great for events, promos and corporate gifts.

Type any name, in English or Arabic. Long names shrink to fit.
